# Regulated credit cooperative specialization — feasibility gate

Research snapshot: 2026-11-24; Brazil is the first jurisdiction investigated,
not a default jurisdiction for the core. Evidence base:
`25096a5e570d5a65d763ad3d0e1b4dc3799e4b1d`.
This is an initial applicability matrix, not a legal opinion, regulatory
certification, bank implementation or permission to operate. Confirm the
current official consolidated text, effective-date amendments and applicability
with the licensed institution and its legal, risk and accounting owners before
specifying operational interfaces.

## Product boundary

Ordinary cooperative accounting (#62) may manage member capital, admission,
contributions, production, allocation of surplus and ordinary commercial
receivables. A member's capital contribution is not a demand deposit or
loan. Connecting a cooperative's own bank account to read statements is not
providing deposit accounts or credit to members. Distinguish the cooperative's
own accounting and cash management from the liabilities, credit portfolios,
risk systems, regulatory capital and reporting of a regulated institution.

Brazil's [LC 130/2009](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp130.htm),
as amended by [LC 196/2022](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp196.htm),
subjects credit cooperatives to financial-system regulation and distinguishes
singular cooperatives, centrals, credit confederations and service
confederations. Financial mutuality has member-scope restrictions and defined
statutory exceptions; service confederations do not acquire authorization for
operations reserved to financial institutions. An ordinary mixed cooperative
cannot simply enable a credit section. Entity category, permitted operation,
authorization and affiliation must be independently recorded.

This matrix does not enumerate every product-specific rule (such as specific

statutes/DOU/Sisbacen take precedence over portal excerpts. Recheck the entire
matrix at qualification, including superseded and future-effective clauses.

## Core reuse, separate ownership and synthetic examples

The current code provides `LedgerService` journal/account and reversal,
`AccessService` workspace role/capability checks,
`SaasPolicyRuntimeGate`, `PaymentWorkflowService` for commercial
invoice/obligation settlement, `ActivityFeedService` and import
reconciliation. These are general finance building blocks, not a regulated
deposit or credit portfolio. Future exact currency, period/closing,
dimensions, audit, extension and financial-report contracts are dependencies.

An optional specialization would own member financial-product contracts,
product eligibility, deposit/loan subledger identities, schedules, rates,
arrears, allowances, provisioning and risk metadata, and link immutable
posted events to one core ledger. An externally managed authoritative
institution system remains the source of truth; import evidence and
reconcile authorized records, never simulate successful movement of money.
A separate jurisdiction-specific reporting adapter would own rule versions
and official mappings, rather than rewriting universal accounts. No new
app or endpoint is proposed before those boundaries are approved.

Synthetic acceptance scenarios (not regulatory formulae):

- An ordinary member contributes BRL 100.00: under an illustrative equity
  classification, debit cash 100 / credit member capital 100. A separate
  licensed deposit product with the same cash receipt instead credits a
  deposit liability; ordinary cooperative workflows cannot create it.
- A synthetic licensed-loan portfolio disburses BRL 1,000 and receives
  principal BRL 200 plus interest BRL 20; expected loan principal balance
  800, cash receipts 220 and interest income 20. Separately authorized
  illustrative loss allowance of 40 yields net carrying amount 760 without
  changing principal. Actual valuation, accounting basis and loss formula
  must be supplied and verified by qualified owners.
- Duplicate external event: one ledger effect, stable portfolio reference;
  failed validation leaves no partial posting. Denied operation for entity
  category, suspended authorization, foreign workspace or revoked operator
  creates neither product contract nor journal.
- For a dated synthetic report mapping, sum permitted account balances
  independently, compare to trial balance, reject missing/invalid rubrics,
  detect a stale mapping and never overwrite a prior approved snapshot.
  Official validation is unclaimed until the authorized institution verifies
  the supported version and receives evidence from a permitted environment.

## Go/no-go and phased gate

**Go:** independent cooperative capital and general accounting; synthetic
research, read-only interchange and reconciliation prototypes, without live
regulated operations. A feasible institution-specific pilot requires written
scope from an authorized institution and qualified accounting/risk/compliance
owners; verified category, products, segment, legal and data responsibilities;
selected official source/mapping versions; safe synthetic fixtures and
access to an explicitly authorized test environment.

**No-go:** enabling deposits, credit origination, payment/clearing,
prudential production calculations, regulatory reporting/submission or
advertising a banking product today. The current core has no independently
qualified regulated product, risk model, official Cosif mapping or regulator
integration. Advance only after a reviewed contractual design, independent
golden portfolio/ledger and rule tests, full database/concurrency,
authorization/security and recovery validation, institutional assessment
and a separate release/qualification decision. Do not create speculative
repositories or burden ordinary international finance with local rules.
